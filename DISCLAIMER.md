# Disclaimer

## Not legal advice

AIGRCeler is a tool for organizing and recording AI governance work. It is not legal, regulatory, audit or professional advice, and using it does not make a company compliant with any law, standard or contract.

- The map from controls to ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act and the NIST AI RMF 1.0 is **indicative**. It shows where a control helps with an item. It never says that a company meets the item.
- What a law or regulator requires of your company is a question for your own counsel. AIGRCeler records what the company says and decides, and leaves the confirmation to qualified people.
- The starting values (retention periods, incident clocks, risk bands, evidence validity, lane rules, legal mappings) are provisional. They are listed in `aigrceler/.claude/shared/methodology/OPEN-ITEMS.md` with what to check each one against. Check them before you rely on them.

## People decide

The agents draft, ask, analyze, score and record. They can be wrong. Every approval, every accepted risk and every start of a stage belongs to a named person in the company, and the engine will not record one without that person's own words. A statement alone does not prove that a control works.

## Your data

The records stay in files on your computer. The agents run in Claude Code, so what you type or paste into a session goes to the AI service you use with it, under that service's terms and your own settings. You are responsible for deciding what may be put into a session.

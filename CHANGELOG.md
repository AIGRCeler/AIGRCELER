# Changelog

All notable changes to AIGRCeler are listed here, newest first. Versions follow semantic versioning: the first number changes when a company's existing records or a command, agent, stage or control ID would break, the second when something is added and old records still work, the third for fixes and wording.

Lines under "Unreleased" start with one of four words: Breaking, Added, Changed, Fixed. The release tool compares them with what it measures in the product.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-10-27
- Added: first public version. Four stages (Discover, Assess, Govern, Operate; Operate never closes), eleven agents and eleven skills that work in Claude Code, and an engine written in Python's standard library.
- Added: a starter catalog of 104 controls in seven areas, 32 risk scenarios, a 77-question discovery profile, and a map of where the controls meet ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act and the NIST AI RMF. The ISO/IEC 42001 map names clauses by number and short title only and does not reproduce the standard.
- Added: a decision journal that cannot be edited without detection, evidence with fingerprints and expiry dates, and a governance dashboard built by the engine from a fixed template. The dashboard shows the AIGRCeler logo (light and dark versions, inside the page, no outside files): in the footer always, and in the header when the company has set no logo of its own.
- Added: a self-test of the engine (hundreds of checks) shipped with the product.
- Added: ownership and contact notices: the README names the copyright holder (H&G Tech Inc), says that the licence does not give a right to use the AIGRCeler name or logo, and lists the website, YouTube and LinkedIn addresses; the disclaimer says who the name belongs to; the security notice gives a mailbox as a second way to report a problem.

# AI Governance charter (starting point)

Company: ______  Date: ______

**Who.** AI Governance is: ______ (person or group), with ______ as deputy. Contact: ______.

**Context.** What the company does and where AI is used: ______. Who has an interest in how it is governed (customers, staff, regulators, partners, owners) and what they expect: ______. Why AI Governance exists and what it is meant to achieve: ______.

**Scope.** This charter covers: ______ (which parts of the company, which AI systems, whether AI tools bought from vendors are in or out). How it relates to other company policies (data protection, security, procurement, acceptable use): ______.

**Authority.** This charter is approved by ______ (board, owner, managing director) and is the basis on which AI Governance acts.

**AI Governance may:** approve and finalize controls for AI systems; own and change the control catalog; start and close stages; confirm lanes; grant exceptions; waive or retire controls; overrule a finding; accept residual risk up to ______; close incidents.

**AI Governance never:** implements a control, except as a recorded exception with an end date; accepts a risk above its threshold (executive leadership does).

**Executive leadership** accepts residual risk at the highest level, alone or together with AI Governance.

**Owners** (people in roles) implement controls and send proof. No one approves their own control.

**Records.** Decisions are recorded in the decision journal in the decision-maker's own words. The journal is kept for ______ years.

Reviewed every ______ months.

Signed: ______

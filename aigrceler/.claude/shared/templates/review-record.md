# Review record

System: ______  Kind: periodic / post-incident / audit / regulatory  Date: ______  Reviewer: ______

1. What changed since the last review (data, model, vendor, use, users, law)?
2. Controls: how many verified, expired, rejected, waived? Any that keep failing?
3. Findings AI Governance overruled: what do they say about the criteria?
4. Incidents and near misses: lessons.
5. Risk: inherent and residual by dimension; any acceptance that should lapse?
6. People: late requests, owner changes, training.
7. Chase list: what is late, with whom, and what happens next (`operate chase`).
8. Lane: still right? Spot-check result for light systems.
9. Next review date.

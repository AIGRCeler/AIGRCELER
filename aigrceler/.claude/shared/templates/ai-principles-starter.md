# AI principles (starting point: adapt to your company)

We use AI to serve our customers and our staff, not to replace judgment where people can be harmed.

1. **Valid and reliable.** We test before we rely, and we keep checking.
2. **Safe.** We design so that a failure does not hurt people, and so we can stop the system quickly.
3. **Secure and resilient.** We protect the system, its data and its keys, and we test it as an attacker would.
4. **Accountable and transparent.** A named person answers for every system. People know when they deal with AI.
5. **Explainable.** People affected by a decision can ask why and ask for a person to look again.
6. **Private.** We use only the personal data we need, for a stated reason, for as long as we need it.
7. **Fair.** We look for unfair differences in how groups are treated, and we fix them.
